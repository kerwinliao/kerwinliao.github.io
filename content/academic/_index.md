I am an independent researcher working across critical social theory, organization theory, and philosophy. My research examines how meritocratic, competitive institutions manufacture relational and emotional scarcity, and how that scarcity sustains itself. I draw on Frankfurt School critical theory, especially work on recognition and acceleration.
